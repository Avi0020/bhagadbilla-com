How an intraday call is built.

  1. Scan the market

    It reads 5-minute and 15-minute candles on Nifty 50, Bank Nifty and large F&O stocks during NSE hours.

  2. Pick the contract

    It chooses a call or put, the expiry and a strike that is liquid enough to trade.

  3. Check the live price

    It reads the real bid, ask, volume and open interest. Contracts with a wide spread or no trading are blocked.

  4. Set the levels

    Trigger, stop-loss and targets come from volatility, so the risk on one lot is known before you enter.

  5. Practise, then decide

    Paper-trade the call, watch how it behaves, then decide whether to trade it yourself.

Questions traders ask.

Where can I get free intraday option calls for Nifty and Bank Nifty?

Open the Bhagad Billa terminal at bhagadbilla.tech. It scans Nifty 50, Bank Nifty and F&O stocks during market hours and lists option calls with the contract, live bid and ask, trigger, stop-loss and targets. Try them with paper money first.

Are the intraday calls guaranteed to make money?

No. No intraday call can be guaranteed. Options can lose their entire premium. Bhagad Billa labels its signals as experimental, tests them on live prices with paper money and counts spread, brokerage and taxes, and does not treat a signal as proven until it has at least 60 closed paper trades.

What does an intraday option call on Bhagad Billa include?

Each call names the exact contract (underlying, expiry, strike, CE or PE), the live bid, ask and spread where the broker feed provides them, a trigger price, a stop-loss, targets, the risk-to-reward ratio and the profit or loss for one lot if the levels are hit.

Which markets does it cover?

Indian markets: Nifty 50, Bank Nifty and large F&O stocks such as Reliance, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Infosys, TCS, SBI, ITC, L&T and Axis Bank, during NSE market hours.

Can I practise before using real money?

Yes. Paper trading uses real market prices with virtual money, fills at the ask and exits at the bid, and counts charges, so your practice results are not flattered.
